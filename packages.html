Recipe Sheet Mobile App

Upload and convert recipes from pictures, spreadsheets, or documents into one consistent recipe style.

Spreadsheet headers: name,category,prep_minutes,cook_minutes,servings,ingredients,steps,notes,image_url,ingredient_costs,currency

1) Upload your source

Supported: CSV/XLS/XLSX, TXT/MD/JSON/DOCX, and images (OCR).

2) Spreadsheet URL or pasted recipe text

3) Browse by category + total cost
